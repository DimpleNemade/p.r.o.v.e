# Glossary

- Artifact: a normalized record derived from registered evidence.
- Chain of custody: chronological record of controlled evidence actions.
- Finding: examiner-authored statement with explicit supporting sources and review state.
- Provenance: traceable relationship between source evidence and a derived record.
- Processing run: one execution of a named/versioned processor.
- Observed evidence: directly recorded source fact, not an examiner conclusion.
- Normalized interpretation: structured representation produced by a processor.
- Machine-generated suggestion: future labeled output requiring examiner review.
- Examiner-approved conclusion: human-controlled finding status; not automatically produced by V0.1.
