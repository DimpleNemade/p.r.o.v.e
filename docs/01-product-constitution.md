# Product constitution

## Principles

1. Evidence integrity precedes convenience.
2. Provenance is a first-class record, not a UI decoration.
3. Observations, normalized interpretations, machine suggestions, and approved conclusions remain distinct.
4. Examiner control is explicit.
5. Unsupported capability is disclosed rather than implied.
6. Audit and custody records are append-only at the application boundary.

The platform does not claim legal admissibility, court readiness, parser completeness, or production forensic validation.
